Title: Regional Brain Metabolic Signatures of Dementia with Lewy Bodies: A Multimodal Radiomics Analysis Integrating FDG-PET, sMRI, and Biological (CSF/Blood) Confirmation of Synucleinopathy
Proposal Summary
The central diagnostic and pathophysiological question in dementia with Lewy bodies (DLB) is how regional brain metabolism relates to underlying Lewy body pathology and its clinical expression. FDG-PET hypometabolism patterns — particularly occipital and posterior cortical involvement with relative sparing of the posterior cingulate ("cingulate island sign") — are among the most specific imaging correlates of DLB, yet the relationship between these regional metabolic changes and the burden, distribution, and biological confirmation of Lewy body pathology (via CSF/blood α-synuclein and related markers) remains incompletely characterized at the radiomic, sub-regional level.
This project's main objective is to characterize regional brain glucose metabolism in DLB using FDG-PET radiomics, and to determine how these metabolic patterns relate to biologically confirmed Lewy body pathology and structural brain changes. CSF, blood biomarkers, and sMRI serve as supporting modalities to anchor and validate the metabolic findings against pathological status and atrophy, rather than as independent classification targets.
