There's no dedicated online proposal portal — E-DLB runs data access through direct contact rather than a self-service system. Here's where to go:

**Primary contact:**
- Email: **edlb.consortium@gmail.com** (listed as the main "if you'd like to join / have questions" contact)
- Secondary/administrative: **sesam@sus.no** (SESAM, Stavanger University Hospital — the coordinating center)

**Who to address it to:**
The consortium is coordinated out of SESAM (Centre for Age-Related Medicine, Stavanger University Hospital, Norway), with Dag Aarsland as PI of the European DLB Consortium. Your proposal would go to the E-DLB steering committee, which reviews data access requests and arranges material transfer agreements — there's no automated portal like ADNI's online DUA system, it's a direct application process.

**What to check before writing your proposal:**
The site has an "About E-DLB" and "Study Design" page (https://www.e-dlb.com/about-edlb/ and https://www.e-dlb.com/study_design/) that may outline the formal proposal template or requirements — worth a quick look before you draft anything, since consortiums like this often want a structured concept form (aims, requested variables/modalities, PI credentials, ethics approval status) rather than a free-text email.
